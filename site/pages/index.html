Animated icons

Icons that show what your home is doing.

Every common Home Assistant device gets an icon that follows its state. The washer spins while it runs, the blind lowers in 10% steps, the lock turns red when it jams. Icons only move while something is happening, and stop when Reduce Motion is on.

    Area moments

    Moments, not a wall of scenes.

    Each area has a default, like a time-of-day scene or a motion automation. A few moments override it for a while: Movie, Reading, Dinner. One chip is lit at a time, and it stays lit only while the lights still match.

    • A moment runs scenes, scripts, automations or buttons, in order.
    • Tap a lit chip to turn it off: its scripts stop and its lights go off.
    • Your names, your order. Nothing imported wholesale from Hue.

    Built on areas and floors

    One line of config. The area does the rest.

    Give a card an area and it finds that area's lights, temperature, humidity, air quality, presence and alarms on its own. Override any part, or hide it with false.

    • Area headers with readings, red alert chips and an all-lights switch.
    • Area cards for the Home view, over a faded photo of the area.
    • Floor headings that sum up who's where and what's on.

    Building blocks

    Fifteen cards. Your dashboard.

    Moments doesn't take over your dashboard. Mix its cards with stock cards in any view or section, and follow the same layout in every area so you always know where to look.

    Open the gallery for every card and every option, with YAML you can paste.

    Made for touch

    Big targets, haptics in the Home Assistant app, and layouts that sit side by side on a tablet or desktop and stack on a phone.

    Light and dark

    A Moments theme with warm amber for “on”, eight backdrops, and solid, frosted or glass tiles. The cards look right in any theme too.

    Plain YAML

    Every option is optional and every default is sensible. Anything a device can't report is simply left out.

    Get started

    Installed in a few minutes.

    Download

    Add Moments from Home Assistant, or copy ha-moments.js from the latest release into /config/www.

    Compose

    Put your areas on floors, then paste the example Home and area views and swap in your own entities.

    Make it yours

    Add the theme and a backdrop, choose Glow or Crisp, and set it as the default dashboard on each device.
